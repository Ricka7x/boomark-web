Questions, answered.

Is Boomark a subscription?

No. It's $7, once. No recurring charge, no tiers to upgrade into later.

Does the browser extension cost extra?

No. The Mac app, the extension, and the iOS app are all part of the same $7 purchase.

Which browsers does the extension work with?

Any of them. It adds the page you're on to Boomark without making you leave the tab.

What happens to a pinned bookmark if I reassign its shortcut?

It stays pinned. Pinning and the ⌘1–⌘5 shortcuts are tracked separately, so reassigning one doesn't touch the other.

What do I need to run it?

A Mac on macOS 13 or later. The iOS app mirrors the same list once you're set up.
